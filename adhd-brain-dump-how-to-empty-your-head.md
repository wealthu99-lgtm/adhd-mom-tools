## Why your head feels so full

Most moms carry an invisible second job: remembering the permission slip, the dentist call, the birthday gift, the almost-empty laundry detergent, and everything else. With ADHD, working memory has less room to hold it all, so the list loops in your head instead of staying put.

A brain dump gives those thoughts somewhere else to live. Once they are on a page or screen, your brain can stop rehearsing them.

## How to do an ADHD brain dump in 10 minutes

1. **Set a 5-minute timer.** A short limit makes it easier to start.
2. **Write everything, unfiltered.** Tasks, worries, random ideas, things you feel guilty about. Do not fix the spelling. Do not decide what matters yet.
3. **Stop when the timer ends.** You can dump again later. You do not need to be complete.
4. **Sort what you wrote.** Put each item into one of four groups:
   - **Do today:** has a deadline today or tomorrow, or something will go wrong if it waits.
   - **This week:** needs doing soon but not today.
   - **Someday:** nice to do, no deadline.
   - **Let go:** worries, “shoulds,” and things you cannot control.
5. **Choose one or two items from “Do today.”** Not the whole list. Just one or two.
6. **Shrink the first one.** Turn it into the smallest possible first step, then give it a five-minute timer.

## What to do with feelings and worries

A brain dump often includes things that are not tasks: “I feel like I’m failing,” “What if I forget again?” Do not force these into your to-do list.

- **Feelings:** write them down and leave them there. Naming them is enough for now.
- **Worries:** ask, “Is there one tiny thing I can do about this?” If yes, add that one thing as a task. If no, park it.

## Brain dump vs. to-do list

- **A brain dump** holds tasks, worries, feelings and ideas, in no particular order. Its goal is to empty your head. Use it when you feel overwhelmed.
- **A to-do list** holds only tasks, in priority order. Its goal is to plan your day. Use it when you already know what needs doing.

Brain dump first, then build a short to-do list from it.
