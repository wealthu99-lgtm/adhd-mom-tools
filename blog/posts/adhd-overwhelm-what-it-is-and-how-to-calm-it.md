## What overwhelm is

Overwhelm is what happens when the demands in front of you feel bigger than your ability to hold and sort them. With ADHD, working memory and task initiation are often harder, so the pile can feel impossible to start. Moms often feel it most when many people need something at the same moment.

## 7 ways to calm ADHD overwhelm

1. **Pause and breathe.** Breathe in for 4 and out for 6, five times. It tells your body it is not an emergency.
2. **Get it out of your head.** Write everything down without sorting. A [brain dump](/adhd-brain-dump/) separates tasks from worries and feelings for you.
3. **Choose only one thing.** Not the most important. The easiest or the most urgent.
4. **Shrink the first step.** "Do the dishes" becomes "put five things in the dishwasher."
5. **Lower the noise.** Turn off the TV, silence notifications, step into a quieter room.
6. **Ask for one specific thing.** "Can you take the kids for 20 minutes?" works better than "I need help."
7. **Lower the bar for today.** Doing the minimum on a hard day is a skill, not a failure.

## Prevent the pile-up

Overwhelm often builds when open tasks stay in your head. Choose three priorities each day with [Today's 3 Musts](/todays-3-musts/) and keep everything else on a list you trust.

## When to get more help

If overwhelm is constant, or comes with low mood, anxiety or thoughts of hurting yourself, please talk to a doctor or therapist. In the US you can call or text 988 at any time. This post is general information, not medical advice.
