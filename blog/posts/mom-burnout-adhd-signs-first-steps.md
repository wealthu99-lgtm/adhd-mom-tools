## What mom burnout can look like

Burnout is not just being tired. People describe it as exhaustion that sleep does not fix, mixed with other signs:

- A short fuse, or crying more easily than usual
- Feeling numb, detached or "on autopilot" with your kids
- Dreading the day before it starts
- Feeling like you are failing no matter how hard you try
- Headaches, aches or getting sick more often

Researchers use the term parental burnout for this kind of long-running exhaustion in caregiving. These signs are common descriptions, not a diagnosis.

## Why ADHD can make it heavier

Parenting asks for constant planning, remembering and switching tasks, which are skills ADHD can make harder. Carrying that load for years, often while masking or trying to "do it right," can wear a person down. The answer is not trying harder. It is changing the load.

## First steps that are small enough to actually do

1. **Cut one thing this week.** One commitment, one chore standard, one "should."
2. **Share or outsource one thing.** Give a person full ownership of one area, like all school forms, or automate a recurring bill or grocery order. See our guide to the [mom mental load](/blog/mom-mental-load-and-adhd-how-to-lighten-it/).
3. **Protect 20 minutes of rest.** Put it on the calendar like an appointment.
4. **Shrink your day.** Choose just [three priorities](/todays-3-musts/) and let the rest wait.
5. **Tell one person the truth.** A friend, partner or family member. Being seen can lighten the weight.

If you cannot even start, the [What Should I Do Now? helper](/what-should-i-do-now/) gives you one tiny step.

## When to get professional help

Please talk to a doctor or therapist if the exhaustion lasts for weeks, affects your daily life, or comes with hopelessness or anxiety. If you are pregnant or have recently had a baby and feel this way, tell your doctor or midwife. If you might hurt yourself or someone else, call or text 988 in the US, or contact your local emergency number.

This post is general information, not medical advice.
