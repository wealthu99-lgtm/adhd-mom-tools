## What the mental load is

The mental load is the thinking behind the doing: remembering the permission slip, noticing the milk is low, knowing which kid needs a haircut. It is the work nobody sees. When you have ADHD, it can feel heavier because holding many open tasks in your head takes more effort and things slip even when you try hard.

## 6 ways to lighten it

1. **Build an external brain.** Write everything down in one place. A [brain dump](/adhd-brain-dump/) gets it out and sorts it into tasks, worries and feelings.
2. **Share one list.** Use one shared calendar and one shared list with your partner, so you do not have to remember to tell them.
3. **Give away ownership, not tasks.** "You own all dentist appointments" is more helpful than "can you book the dentist?"
4. **Create defaults.** A simple weekly dinner rotation, a fixed laundry day and a launch pad by the door remove decisions.
5. **Batch the decisions.** Do your planning once a week instead of ten times a day. Pick a time, review the list and set [your three priorities](/todays-3-musts/).
6. **Lower the standard.** Some things can be "good enough." Not everything needs doing at once, or by you.

## A simple script for your partner

"I am carrying too much in my head. I would like you to fully own the kids' appointments and school forms, including remembering them. Can we try that for a month and see how it goes?"

## If you carry most of it alone

Automate what you can: recurring bills, grocery pickup, repeating calendar reminders. Keep the list short and use a daily reset. If overwhelm is high, see our guide to [calming ADHD overwhelm](/blog/adhd-overwhelm-what-it-is-and-how-to-calm-it/).

This post is general information, not medical advice. If the load is affecting your health, please talk to a doctor or therapist.
