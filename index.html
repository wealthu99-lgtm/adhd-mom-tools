Frequently asked questions

What are ADHD Mom Tools?

A free set of seven simple online tools for moms with ADHD: a visual focus timer, a visual timer for kids, a brain dump organizer, a daily top-3 priorities planner, a next-step helper, a time blocker and a routine builder.

Are the tools really free?

Yes. Every tool is free to use with no signup, no ads and no time limit.

Is my information private?

Yes. Everything you type is processed in your browser and saved only on your own device. Nothing is sent to a server.

Are these tools only for moms?

They are designed with ADHD moms in mind, but anyone who feels overwhelmed or struggles with time and focus can use them.

Do these tools replace ADHD treatment?

No. They are practical organization aids, not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. If daily life feels unmanageable, talk to a doctor or therapist who understands ADHD.
